Materials used on a Field Service order (the stock requests on the Inventory tab) are shown in a
**Materials** section on the order's Accounting tab and are added to the customer invoice.

* Outbound, non-cancelled stock requests are taken into account; inbound (return) lines are not.
* Billable quantity: the delivered quantity, or the requested quantity while nothing has been delivered yet.
* Unit price: the pricelist of the invoice partner (same rule as the other invoice lines).
* On the invoice, materials are grouped per product and unit of measure.
